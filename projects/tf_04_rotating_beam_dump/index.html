FRIB · 2024 - Present

Rotating beam dump development

I lead the overall fabrication and installation effort for the FRIB rotating beam dump, running the simulations and working with specialists in materials, systems engineering, thermomechanics, and radiation.

Development target: 100 kW primary beam power by 2028.

Purpose

The rotating beam dump is the next upgrade beyond the static beam dump, intended for primary beam power above 50 kW. Rotation spreads the heat load around the circumference, while a thin shell lets the beam deposit energy directly in the cooling water.

Approach

The project integrates thermal-hydraulic design with radiation, sealing, manufacturing, and installation requirements. I perform thermal-fluid, structural, and electromagnetic simulations.

Ongoing AI methods

The rotating beam dump is the current application for two AI methods in development.

BiSTO

Binary Surrogate-based Topology Optimization uses AI to search solid-fluid layouts for the cooling channels and checks each design with physics-based simulation.

Deep learning surrogate

Predicts the thermal response of the rotating beam dump quickly, so many design options can be checked without a full simulation for each one.
