UNIST · 2014 - 2021

Electromagnetic pumps & MHD generators

I designed and analyzed electromagnetic pumps and investigated magnetohydrodynamic generator performance.

Research scope: Liquid metal circulation and magnetohydrodynamic power generation design studies.

Purpose

This work investigates liquid-metal circulation for reactor and accelerator systems, together with magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) power generation from conductive flows.

Approach

Coupled electromagnetic and flow models compare pump geometries and generator operating conditions. The studies cover sodium and lithium circulation and Faraday-type plasma generators.

DC electromagnetic pumps (2014-2021)

DC electromagnetic pump designs: rectangular type schematic (top left), fabricated pump (top center), helical type schematic (top right), and MHD analysis results (bottom)

Sodium fast reactor applications

I designed and analyzed rectangular, cylindrical, and helical-type DC electromagnetic pumps for liquid sodium circulation, in design studies for the coolant test loop of a prototype Gen-IV sodium-cooled fast reactor. I compared the pump geometries and analyzed their magnetic flux density, current distribution, and Lorentz force.

Heavy-ion accelerator applications

I optimized a rectangular DC electromagnetic pump for liquid lithium circulation in a heavy-ion accelerator. In the numerical analysis, the pump reached a developed pressure of 1.5 MPa at a flow rate of 6 cm³/s, for forming the liquid lithium films used in uranium charge stripping.

MHD generators (2018-2021)

I numerically investigated Faraday-type MHD generators for combined MHD-turbine power generation, including a design study for a 10 kW generator and nitrogen-plasma flow analysis. The work examined electromagnetic, flow, and geometric parameters.
