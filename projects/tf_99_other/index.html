FRIB · 2024 - 2026

Thermal and structural engineering at FRIB

I performed thermal-fluid and structural analyses to support design reviews, set operating limits, and qualify devices at FRIB.

Engineering studies: Simulation studies and experimental comparisons for shielding, plasma-chamber, charge-selector, and target systems.

Purpose

Beam-intercepting devices, shielding, and target systems require thermal management and structural assessment as FRIB increases beam power.

Approach

Heat-deposition inputs inform thermal-fluid and stress models. Cross-code comparisons and, where available, measurements help assess operating limits and assembly conditions.

Post-target shielding (2025-2026)

As FRIB increases beam power, the post-target shielding (PTS) needs an optimized cooling-channel design and thermal stress analysis. The heat distribution calculated from PHITS Monte Carlo simulations was coupled with COMSOL to minimize stress and develop a practical design for field implementation.

Post-target shielding analysis: cooling channel geometry, temperature distribution, and von Mises stress

Plasma chamber (2025-2026)

Thermal and structural analyses in ANSYS and COMSOL were compared to assess RF-power thermal limits. Transient analysis also evaluated the temperature and assembly time needed for interference fitting of the cooling-channel components.

Plasma-chamber temperature predictions compared between ANSYS and COMSOL.

Low-power charge selector (2025-2026)

I performed transient and stationary thermal simulations of the low-power charge selector (LPCS) to determine power limits based on thermal stress. I compared the simulations with measured temperatures to estimate the beam power deposited on the drift tube.

Simulated and measured temperatures used to estimate beam energy deposition.

Target system (2024-2025)

I performed system-level thermal analysis for the graphite-based rotating target, including the effects of thermal radiation on the target door cooling and flow rate requirements. Simulations and experiments were conducted to evaluate the thermal conductance and operational limits of the bearing system.
