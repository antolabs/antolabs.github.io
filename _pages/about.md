bio_paragraphs:
  - Engineering devices cannot keep growing as power and energy demands rise, so more performance must come from better design. My goal is an AI design method that needs only standard simulations and still works when the physics changes, so that engineers can design, print, and test their own devices and monitor them in operation with digital twins. AI searches for designs, a physics solver checks each one, and hardware tests check the solver. My background spans thermal, fluid, electromagnetic, magnetohydrodynamic (MHD), and structural analysis. BiSTO for turbulent cooling channels is the first step, and I am now generalizing it to 3D.
