Research & Projects

Generalizable AI Methods for Multiphysics Design

My work spans thermal, fluid, structural, electromagnetic, and magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) systems, from surrogate guided optimization and coupled simulation to metal additive manufacturing and loop testing.

Research Framework

BiSTO is the core engine: an AI model ranks changes to a layout, and a physics solver checks the best ones, so the method needs only standard simulations. I am now generalizing it to 3D. I plan to test printed designs in a benchtop loop, apply the same method to electromagnetic pumps and liquid metal MHD, and then use test data to correct the models and build digital twins that detect abnormal behavior.
