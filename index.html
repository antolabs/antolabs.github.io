Geunhyeong Lee

Scientist, Facility for Rare Isotope Beams (FRIB) · Michigan State University

Engineering devices cannot keep growing as power and energy demands rise, so more performance must come from better design. My goal is an AI design method that needs only standard simulations and still works when the physics changes, so that engineers can use it from design to operation. AI searches for designs, a physics solver checks each one, and hardware tests check the solver.
